# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome newcomers, assume good faith, and don't harass,
demean or exclude anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this
code, and may ban participants for behaviour they judge harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private "Report content" option on the offending item, or contact
the maintainers through the address listed on their GitHub profiles. Reports are handled
confidentially.
